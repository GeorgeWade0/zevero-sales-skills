## 1. Company and product

**What Zevero is:** an *expert-led carbon management platform*. It combines AI-assisted Scope 1–3 emissions tracking with in-house sustainability experts, so teams move from measurement to reduction. (N: Positioning – Feb 2026)

**What it isn't:** a generic ESG platform, a pure carbon-accounting tool, a reporting-only compliance tool, or a consultancy.

**External framing:** four capability pillars (use these, not internal module names). (D: 2026 Positioning Strategy)
1. Operational climate data
2. Decision-ready insights: hotspots and prioritised actions
3. Expert-guided execution
4. Reusable outputs: "one dataset, many outcomes"

**Internal modules:**
- Corporate Carbon Footprint Platform
- AI-powered ESG and B Corp Reporting
- PCF Module
- EPD Generation
- Climate strategy advisory: decarbonisation, SBTi, target setting
- **Zevero Lite:** the entry option. There's no free trial. (D: Positioning Strategy; D: Pricing FAQ)

**Platform capabilities:** (N: Platform Manual)
- OCR AutoFill and Excel bulk upload
- Period Lock
- Historical data
- ISO 14064-1 report
- Disclosure reports for B Corp, GRI, SGX and SSBJ
- Emission Engine with semantic factor matching
- NetSuite and Unleashed ERP integrations. These win deals with prospects already on those systems. (N: Marketing Strategy)

**Company facts:** pre-Series A, $14M raised, about 50 people, 400% YoY revenue growth, 20+ countries. Acquired Inhabit (sustainability advisory). Zevero Ltd (UK) is owned by Zevero Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). (N: Marketing Strategy 2026; N: Company Information) ⚠️ Check with marketing before using any of these externally.

**Go-to-market:** sales-led only. Every entry is via Book a Demo; there's no self-serve.

## 4. Value proposition and differentiators

**Core line:** "Zevero takes the manual burden out of carbon management and gives teams a practical way to turn emissions data into action, with software for scale and experts for the decisions that matter." (D: Positioning Strategy)

**The wedge:** in a world where every platform has AI, the differentiator is who stands behind the output. Our experts share accountability for the numbers.

| Against | Our angle |
|---|---|
| Spreadsheets / DIY | No rebuilding every year. Continuous tracking instead of a snapshot. An auditable system of record. |
| Consultancies | A one-off footprint is a snapshot; Zevero tracks progress year-round. Compare the cost with a retainer or a hire. You own your data. |
| Software-only tools | Scope 3 at every tier (others gate it). Target setting from the entry tier (Greenly locks reduction trajectories to its top plan). Named experts behind the numbers. |

⚠️ **Expert access by tier is unconfirmed.** One source says experts are standard; others say 1x/2x/4x expert touchpoints a year by tier, with a dedicated expert only at Premium. Don't claim "dedicated expert" below Premium until this is confirmed.

**Fit angles by situation:** see `discovery-brief/references/zevero_fit_angles.md`.

## 9. Sales process and methodology

**Default qualification method: MEDDPICC.** Zevero uses a simplified version. (N: ICP & Qualification)

| Letter | What we need |
|---|---|
| **M**etrics | Their cost or risk in numbers: consultant spend, hours, tender value at risk, CBAM exposure |
| **E**conomic buyer | Named. UK: CFO or CEO. Japan: whoever owns the ringi. |
| **D**ecision criteria | What they'll judge on: methodology defensibility, expert access, Scope 3 depth, integrations |
| **D**ecision process | Steps, people and dates to signature |
| **P**aper process | Procurement, security review (Trust Centre), legal, PO |
| **I**dentify pain | The trigger from section 2, in their words |
| **C**hampion | Named, with access and influence. In Japan, never leave discovery without one; ghosting usually means there's no champion. |
| **C**ompetition | Who else they're looking at, including "do nothing", their consultant, or spreadsheets |

## 11. Tone and messaging

**Do:** (N: Company Messaging; N: Sales Process Guide)
- Be clear, specific, credible and practical.
- Write in plain English for busy non-experts.
- Lead with reduction outcomes, and put value in business terms (cost avoided, risk reduced, tenders won).
- Be direct and transparent with UK buyers.

**Phrases to use:**
- "Credible, explainable numbers"
- "Software for the heavy lifting. Humans for the decisions."
- "Spend less time measuring and more time reducing"
- "Our experts share accountability for the output"
- "Work with the data you actually have"

**Don't:**
- Use buzzwords: game-changing, revolutionary, next-generation, all-in-one, seamless.
- Greenwash or moralise ("save the planet").
- Lead with "Powered by AI".
- Use "drive revenue / improve efficiency / make better decisions" headlines.
- Say "we don't have X yet". Frame the gap as a design choice and offer what we do instead.
- Over-promise.

**Format rules:**
- No em dashes in outreach.
- Cold emails under 150 words.
- LinkedIn messages under 80 words.
- UK English for UK and EU prospects.
