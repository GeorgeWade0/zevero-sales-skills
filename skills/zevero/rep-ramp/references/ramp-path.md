# Zevero rep ramp path

Default programme. Managers can adjust the timing but not the certifications.

## Week 1: Zevero and the market

- **Read:**
  - `zevero-sales-context`, all of it
  - The customer stories at zevero.earth/customer-stories
  - The Trust Centre
- **Shadow** 3 live calls, and run `call-summary` on each one as practice.
- **Install** your role kit and run `setup` to connect HubSpot, Gmail, Calendar and Granola.
- **Checkpoint:** explain to your manager in 2 minutes:
  - What Zevero is
  - Who we sell to
  - The expert-plus-platform wedge
  - Our top 3 competitors and their landmine questions

## Week 2: Discovery

- **Skills:**
  - `discovery-brief`
  - `call-prep`
  - `sales-discovery-questions`
  - `meddpicc-scorecard`
  - `buyer-roleplay`, discovery scenario, at least 3 rounds
- **Japan:** also do the Japan consensus role-play and review the `en-ja-translator` glossary.
- **Certification 1, Discovery:** pass a `buyer-roleplay` discovery call in Certification mode.

## Week 3: Objections, competition and value

- **Skills:**
  - `handle-objection`
  - `battle-card`
  - `deal-value-calc`
  - `value-follow-up-pdf`
  - `buyer-roleplay`, pricing pushback and competitive scenarios
- **Checkpoint:** a manager-approved value case for one real or sample prospect, with no invented numbers.

## Week 4: Live deals with a safety net

**SDR**
- Skills: `prospect`, `cold-email`, `linkedin-outreach`, `sequence-load`
- Target: 50 researched accounts and 2 meetings booked

**AE**
- Run 2 discovery calls with the manager on the call.
- Skills: `call-summary`, `hubspot-post-meeting-update`, `follow-up-48h`, `deal-review`

**AM**
- Skills: `account-context`, `customer-health`, `qbr`, `renewal-radar`
- Prepare one QBR.

**Certification 2, Live call:** one real call reviewed with `sales-call-review`, scoring ≥3 in every area.

## Weeks 5–8: Own a book

- **AE:**
  - Proposals: `sales-proposal`, `close-plan`, `mutual-action-plan`
  - Negotiation: `negotiation-concession-planner`
- **Everyone:**
  - A weekly `deal-review` or `customer-health` with the manager.
  - Run `request-skill` for anything that's slow or missing.
- **Cleared for a full quota** when both certifications have passed and the week-4 targets have been hit.
