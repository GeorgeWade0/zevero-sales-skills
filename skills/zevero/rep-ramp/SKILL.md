# Rep ramp

Builds and tracks a new rep's ramp. Read `zevero-sales-context` first, and use [references/ramp-path.md](references/ramp-path.md) as the default programme.

## Build a plan

Ask for:
- **Rep and role:** SDR, AE or AM.
- **Market:** UK, Japan or APAC.
- **Start date and manager.**

Then produce the plan:
1. Take the ramp path for that role and put real dates on it.
2. Add the market adjustments. Japan reps add the Japan consensus role-play and the `en-ja-translator` glossary review. UK and APAC reps skip both.
3. For each week, list the skills to learn, the practice reps, and the checkpoint the manager signs off.

Offer to save the plan as a doc, or as a HubSpot task list for the manager.

## Track progress

When asked where a rep is, collect evidence before judging:
- Role-play scores the rep or manager pastes in.
- `sales-call-review` results from real calls.
- Their activity and deals in HubSpot, if connected.

Report three things:
- Each checkpoint, marked done, behind or at risk.
- The single next thing to unblock.
- Whether they're cleared for live deals. They're cleared only when every certification in the path has passed.

## Certification

Certification means passing a `buyer-roleplay` in Certification mode, plus one real or recorded call reviewed with `sales-call-review`. The manager confirms both. Never mark a certification passed without a score or review shown in this conversation.
