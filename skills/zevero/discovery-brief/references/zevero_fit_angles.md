# Zevero fit — common angles

Use these as a starting point for the "Where Zevero fits" section of the brief. Pick the rows that genuinely apply to what you found in the research. Don't list angles that don't fit — a thin, specific section beats a padded generic one.

## Carbon measurement maturity

| Customer signal | Zevero angle |
|---|---|
| No public Scope 1–3 disclosure | Get them to a defensible baseline footprint — first time, done properly |
| Spreadsheet / DIY measurement | Move off spreadsheets onto an auditable system of record |
| Spend-based Scope 3 | Shift to supplier-specific data for material categories |
| Consultancy-led annual exercise | Real-time platform — own the data internally, year-round, not just once a year |
| Already on Greenly / Watershed / Plan A / Planet Mark | Re-platforming angle: better support, deeper Scope 3, tighter audit trail |

## Standards & certifications

| Customer signal | Zevero angle |
|---|---|
| B Corp recertification under new standards | Defensible Scope 1–3 + reduction roadmap de-risks recert under the climate pillar |
| ISO 14001 certified | System of record for the EMS — repeatable, auditable, less manual at audit time |
| SBTi committed but no baseline | Baseline + tracking to make the commitment credible |
| SBTi pathway considering | Measurement foundation makes SBTi viable |
| CSRD / SECR / Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting | Compliance-ready outputs and audit trail |
| CDP, EcoVadis, customer ESG questionnaires | Single source of truth that feeds external surveys |

## Industry-specific

| Customer signal | Zevero angle |
|---|---|
| Heavy supply chain emissions (food, drink, manufacturing, retail) | Supplier-level data collection over spend-based factors |
| Multi-site operations | Site-level reporting and intensity benchmarking |
| Consumer-facing brand | Product-level footprints and on-pack-ready data |
| Packaging / bottle-weight programmes | Quantify kg CO₂e impact of weight reduction across the portfolio |
| Logistics-heavy operations | Freight-specific emissions modelling with mode/distance/load |

## Cultural & operational fit

| Customer signal | Zevero angle |
|---|---|
| B Corp / mission-driven | Zevero is a mission-aligned peer — you'll work with kindred spirits |
| Sustainability function is small (1–3 people) | Zevero handles the heavy lifting so a small team punches above its weight |
| Sustainability is spread across many teams | Multi-user platform with role-based views beyond the sustainability lead |
| Procurement / operations involvement needed | Supplier engagement workflows beyond the sustainability function |
| Lead is new in seat / recently promoted | Easy win — clean platform handover, shows initiative early in the role |
